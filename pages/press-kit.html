About Pepper Weather

Pepper Weather gives you a fast, clear forecast without the clutter. See today’s outlook and the next few hours at a glance, then explore detailed forecasts whenever you need them—all with no ads. With Premium Standard or Plus, keep the weather handy with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and compare forecasts for different locations side by side.

Key features

  • Compare two locations side by side—including temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity, UV, and a five-day forecast—with instant “warmer by X°” insights
  • See precipitation chances and expected amounts, so you know whether to expect a passing sprinkle or a downpour
  • See how today compares with past years through historical highs, lows, and averages
  • Explore air quality, sunrise and sunset times, daylight trends, and detailed moon forecasts
  • Get notified before precipitation begins and when severe weather alerts are issued in your area
