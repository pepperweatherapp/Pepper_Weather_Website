About Pepper Weather

Pepper Weather is made for those that want a fast, clean forecast, without the clutter. The design is meant to be simple but it has the details you would expect from a powerful weather app. You can see everything from full 10-day outlooks, hourly details, widgets, air quality, and more.

Key features

  • Compare two locations side-by-side — temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity, UV, and a 5-day forecast, with an instant “warmer by X°” comparison
  • True Condition forecasts surface the weather that actually dominates the day, not just one skewed hour
  • True Precipitation chances adjust for how much rain is really expected, so a trace doesn’t read like a downpour
  • See how today compares to past years, with historical highs, lows, and averages
  • Push notifications when severe weather is issued for your area
